BUSINESS MANAGER
Sales, purchase, stock and accounts software for your shop. Works fully offline.

* Your data is stored encrypted in  D:\BusinessManagerData  (or C:\ if there is no D: drive).
  Installing, updating or uninstalling the program never deletes your data.
* The first time you start the software it shows a Machine Code. Send it to the software owner to
  receive your Activation Key.
